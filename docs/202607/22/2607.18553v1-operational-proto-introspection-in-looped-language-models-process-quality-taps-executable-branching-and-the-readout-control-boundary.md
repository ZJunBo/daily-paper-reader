## 摘要
语言模型能否读取正在进行的计算质量？外部干预能否将该读数转化为更好的结果？我们在一个冻结的2.6B循环变换器Ouro-RLTT上检验了这两个问题。在GSM8K上，一个严格的答案前探针排除了答案区域和黄金值，但仍能预测最终成功：隐藏状态加上长度和对数概率捷径达到AUROC 0.797，而仅凭捷径为0.731（增量+0.066；任务聚类95% CI [+0.021, +0.112]；170个任务，680个候选）。低容量探针也能读取角色特定属性：任务不相交分支存活率达到0.9697的oracle保留率，内容排名达到0.6310的宏top-1，生成分支正确性达到AUROC 0.7755。一个非循环控制复制了候选质量读数，因此每个信号都不需要循环。我们基于Ouro的192槽循环缓存构建了分支/携带/剪枝机制，包括特定分支的缓存谱系和逐位精确的残差捕获拼接，仅重新计算受影响的后缀，每个分支层传递节省高达88%。没有冻结的干预产生经过验证的能力增益。方向性引导是一个已确立的负面因素；一个四任务匹配抽样比较消除了冻结分叉增益的证据，但无法估计普遍缺陷；终端选择仍未解决且统计功效不足；有界LoRA改变了表面行为而未改善净可达性。双零审核不支持最简单的跨度错位解释。我们将这种可读取但尚不可用的属性称为操作性原始内省。模型并未咨询我们的探针：我们读取其隐藏轨迹，而我们的干预未能将这些读数转化为验证过的能力。答案前的结果仅限于一个领域。负载值使用来源项不相交分割和适用时的反对称评估。

## Abstract
Can a language model read the quality of ongoing computation, and can an external intervention turn that readout into better outcomes? We test both questions in a frozen 2.6B looped transformer, Ouro-RLTT. On GSM8K, a strict pre-answer probe excludes the answer region and gold value yet predicts eventual success: hidden states plus length and log-probability shortcuts reach AUROC 0.797, versus 0.731 for the shortcuts alone (incremental +0.066; task-clustered 95% CI [+0.021, +0.112]; 170 tasks, 680 candidates). Low-capacity taps also read role-specialized properties: task-disjoint branch survival reaches 0.9697 oracle retention, content ranking reaches 0.6310 macro top-1, and generated-branch correctness reaches AUROC 0.7755. A non-looped control replicates a candidate-quality readout, so recurrence is not required for every signal.   We build branch/carry/prune machinery over Ouro's 192-slot recurrent cache, including branch-specific cache lineage and a bit-exact residual-capture splice that recomputes only the affected suffix and saves up to 88% of per-branch layer passes. No frozen intervention produces a validated capability gain. Directional steering is an established negative; a four-task matched-sampling comparison removes evidence for a frozen-fork gain but cannot estimate a general deficit; terminal selection remains unresolved and underpowered; and bounded LoRA changes surface behavior without improving net reachability. A two-null audit does not support the simplest span-misalignment explanation.   We call this readable-but-not-yet-usable property operational proto-introspection. The model is not consulting our probes: we read its hidden trajectories, and our interventions fail to convert those readouts into validated capability. The pre-answer result is limited to one domain. Load-bearing values use source-item-disjoint splits and antisymmetrized evaluation where applicable.
